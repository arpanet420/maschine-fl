| 8   | 2026-11-05 | FL crashed a few times with BROWSER open or being opened, on a project with Audio Units (Repro-1) | FL's crash log: `plugins.getName(…, FPN_Preset)` from our `read_presets`, through Fruity Wrapper, into FL's Audio Unit wrapper and CoreFoundation.
